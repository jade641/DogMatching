# PawMatch: Data Deletion Instructions

**Last updated: October 17, 2026**

If you want your PawMatch account and all data linked to it deleted, follow the steps below.

## What will be deleted

- Your name, email address, and profile picture from your Facebook or email login
- Your dog profiles and photos
- Your matches and other activity in the App

## How to request deletion

1. Send an email to **jcanlubopaye@gmail.com** with the subject **"PawMatch Data Deletion Request"**.
2. In the email, include the email address or Facebook name you used to log in to PawMatch, so we can find your account.
3. We will delete your data within **7 days** and send you a confirmation email once it is done.

## Remove PawMatch from your Facebook account

You can also remove the App's access to your Facebook account at any time:

1. Open Facebook and go to **Settings & privacy → Settings**.
2. Go to **Apps and Websites** (or **Business Integrations**).
3. Select **PawMatch** and click **Remove**.

Removing the App from Facebook stops it from receiving new information from your Facebook account. To also delete the data already stored in PawMatch, please send the email request described above.
